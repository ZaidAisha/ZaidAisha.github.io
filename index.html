Our Story

      
Aisha & Zaid · from A to Z

    

  


  

    Where do you see yourself in the future?
    
Little did we know that one simple question would lead us to the most beautiful chapter of our lives… one that would eventually end with “Will you marry me?”

  


  

    A

    

      
Chapter one

      
Two Strangers

      
Funny enough, we used to pass by each other all the time, never speaking, never asking, never caring. We didn’t even know each other’s names. Two strangers crossing paths, unaware that one day, our paths would become forever.

    


    

      
Chapter two

      
One Simple Question

      
Then came the day she walked in looking for her friends and happened to sit next to him. Neither of us planned to talk, yet somehow, a simple question changed everything.

      
    


    

      
Chapter three

      
Falling for Each Other

      
From that moment, conversations turned into daily talks, strangers became friends, and somewhere along the way, without even realizing it, we began falling for each other. We never imagined that those little conversations would lead us here, to a love that would become our forever.

      
    


    

      
Chapter four

      
A New Chapter

      
And today, here we are, beginning a new chapter surrounded by the people we love most, celebrating a story that neither of us could have ever imagined.

      
      
    


    Z
  


  

    
Our story started with an A, and it will end with a Z.

    
Not because our love has an ending, but because from A to Z, through every chapter, every challenge, and every beautiful moment, we choose each other. Today, tomorrow, and for all the letters and stories yet to come.

    
And this time, we know exactly where we see ourselves in the future…

    
Together. Forever. ♡
